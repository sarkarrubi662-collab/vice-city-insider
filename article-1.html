GTA 6 Leonida & Vice City Map Breakdown: Every Key Region Explained

Grand Theft Auto 6 brings players back to sun-soaked Vice City, now expanded into the broader, Florida-inspired state of Leonida. Here is a complete breakdown of the distinct regions revealed so far and what they mean for exploration.

1. Downtown Vice City & Ocean Beach

At the heart of the map sits a modern, densely packed reimagining of Vice City. Inspired heavily by Miami, this urban hub blends pastel Art Deco beachfront hotels, crowded boardwalks, high-rise financial towers, and vibrant nightlife districts.

Unlike the 1980s setting of the original classic, modern Vice City features bustling commercial ports, mega-yachts anchored off the coast, and multi-lane elevated expressways that connect the downtown core to surrounding neighborhoods.

2. The Leonida Keys & Island Bridges

Stretching south of the mainland is a tropical chain of islands modeled after the Florida Keys. Connected by long, scenic overwater highways, this region introduces expansive marine gameplay.

  • Watercraft & Aviation Hub: Expect high-speed boat chases, deep-water diving spots, and low-altitude seaplane routes between islands.
  • Chokepoints & Escape Routes: Long bridges create dramatic risk-reward scenarios during high-star police pursuits.

3. The Swamplands & Wetlands

Moving inland away from the neon skyline, Leonida opens up into vast, untamed wetlands inspired by the Florida Everglades. Muddy waterways, dense mangroves, and wildlife—including alligators and flamingos—dominate this ecosystem.

Gameplay Takeaway Hovercrafts, airboats, and lifted off-road trucks will be essential for navigating shallow marshes where standard street vehicles quickly lose traction.

4. Rural Outskirts & Industrial Highways

Beyond the coast and swamps lie small roadside towns, strip malls, dirt racing tracks, and sprawling interstate highways. Official footage highlights mud-bogging meetups, rural gas stations, and gritty backroads that contrast sharply with Vice City's luxury beachfronts.

How Leonida Compares to GTA 5's Los Santos

While GTA 5 centered around a single major city bordered by desert and mountain terrain, Leonida emphasizes interconnected waterways, multiple distinct urban and coastal hubs, and a much higher density of interior and street-level activity.
